## Description to paste

# Happening

Every shelter exit brings an event.

Leave a shelter for a game zone and Happening spawns an event, then announces the map and event in large on-screen text.

Events stay in their stock locations. Punisher, Airdrops, Attack Helicopters, Helicopter Crash Sites, Bogeyman, Driver, and Nomad Gatherings can now appear without day, weekday, night, reputation, chance, or random-delay restrictions. Driver stays on Highway; Nomad Gatherings stay in Village. Fighter Jets are removed.

BTR retains its stock unlocks, probability, and timing, and can appear alongside the guaranteed event. Normal zone-to-zone travel keeps stock scheduling. Stationary trader progression, story events, and random enemy/Nomad groups retain their stock behavior.

## Install

1. Install [Metro Mod Loader](https://modworkshop.net/mod/55623) 3.4.1. Place its two loader files next to `RTV.exe`.
2. Download `Happening.vmz` and put it in the game's `mods` folder.
3. Enable Happening in Metro's Mods tab and launch modded.
4. Leave a shelter and allow the map to initialize. Nomad Gatherings have an additional short setup before their announcement.

Disable Happening in Metro to turn it off.

Tested on Road to Vostok 0.2.0.5 (Build 2) with Metro 3.4.1. Repeated shelter exits have been tested in game; automated event, banner, and hook checks pass. Broader map/event coverage is ongoing. The Events tab's remaining descriptions and unlock labels still show vanilla information.
